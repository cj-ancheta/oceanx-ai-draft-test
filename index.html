Our prototype: AI drafts the judge's feedback

A judge reads an entry and scores it on OceanX's six criteria. After the sixth score, an AI draft of the reason and the feedback appears. The judge edits the draft and saves it. The judge decides every score, and the AI only writes a first draft of the words.

The same screen times each step. Team 7 uses it to test whether drafts save judges time.

How to try it

  1. Click Open the prototype.
  2. Enter the code DEMO, choose group 4 and type any role. Tick both boxes and start.
  3. Give the entry all six scores. The AI draft then appears in the text boxes. Edit it and save.

Group 4 shows AI drafts for the first five entries and no drafts for the last five. The ten drafts were written in advance with one fixed prompt. Your answers stay in your own browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
